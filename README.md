# FamilyConnectionsOfSCSystem
Enterprise Data Management final project working with a non-profit, Family Connections of South Carolina using the Knack platform
# About Family Connection of South Carolina
Family Connection of South Carolina is a statewide nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting families of children with disabilities and special healthcare needs. The organization provides information, referrals, and personalized parent-to-parent support to help families navigate complex medical, educational, and community systems. 
FCSC offers training programs and leadership development opportunities that empower parents to advocate effectively for their children. It collaborates with schools, healthcare providers, and community partners across South Carolina to improve access to services and resources. Through its work, FCSC strengthens families and promotes inclusive communities throughout the state.
Family Connection of South Carolina serves families with children from birth to 26 years old with no eligibility requirements for their services.
# ProjectOverview
The purpose of this project is to build a resource navigation and request management system to replace FCSC's current workflow which is a bit of a hassle and complicated. The way I created this system, it makes the process now smooth, dependent and effecient.
